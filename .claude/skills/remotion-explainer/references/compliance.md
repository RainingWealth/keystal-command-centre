# Compliance rules for every financial-education video
- Insurance outcomes are always conditional: "can help", "depending on the policy", "if an insured key person dies or experiences another covered event". No guarantees, no "you will be paid".
- No insurer names, logos, phone numbers or third-party marks.
- CTA card fine print (26 px minimum): "General information only, not financial advice. Benefits depend on policy terms and conditions."
- Keyman premium tax deduction is CONDITIONAL (IRAS: genuine profit-loss purpose, direct link to key person's profits, business is owner/beneficiary, no cash/investment value, does not wipe out whole profit structure); recoveries can be taxable. Never say "always tax deductible".
- Digital ads: MAS Guidelines on Standards of Conduct for Digital Advertising Activities (in force 2026-03-25) - get institution approval before publishing; Claude never publishes/sends on the user's behalf.
- Synthetic voice only (Kokoro, Apache-2.0); no cloning of real people.
