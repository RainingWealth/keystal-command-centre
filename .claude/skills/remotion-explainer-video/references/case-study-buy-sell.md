# Case study: Buy-Sell Agreement explainer (Singapore SME owners)

Goal: stop a business owner scrolling, make one idea land (agreement defines the transfer; insurance may fund it; both must be structured together) and drive a request for a 15-minute shareholder continuity review. Result: 1080x1920, 30 fps, 57.9 s, 16 MB, -14.0 LUFS / -1.0 dBTP, Whisper sync 108 ms, 129/133 words matched. Cost: $0. Repo: `buy-sell-remotion/` on branch `claude/tender-shannon-lta7ac`, draft PR RainingWealth/keystal-command-centre#6.

| Scene | Time | Narration idea | What the viewer sees | Anchor cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Hook | 0-6.2 s | partner passes away, who owns the shares? | cobalt flash, 50/50 donut with two partners; partner dissolves into particles, half becomes a dashed orange ring; headline "50 / 50" -> "WHO GETS THEIR 50%?" | `passed`, `who` |
| 2 Problem | 6.2-16.9 s | shares may pass to the estate; unplanned co-owners; family needs cash | orphaned share token flies to ESTATE; donut half turns orange; family silhouettes with "?" badges; coin vs struck-out shares; headlines "MAY PASS TO THE ESTATE" -> "UNPLANNED CO-OWNERS?" -> "OWNERSHIP RISK + CASH FLOW PRESSURE"; legal-dependence footnote | `estate`, `sharing`, `people`, `cash`, `notShares` |
| 3 Agreement | 16.9-26.9 s | event, who can buy, price, transfer | signed document builds; cards 01 TRIGGERING EVENT, 02 SHARE VALUATION, 03 OWNERSHIP TRANSFER (ghost card with buyer first); estate -> share token -> buyer "under the agreed terms"; headline "PLAN THE TRANSFER BEFORE A CRISIS" | `event`, `buy`, `price`, `terms`, `transfer` |
| 4 Funding gap | 26.9-34.5 s | an agreement does not give you the cash | "AGREEMENT IN PLACE" chip; obligation bar fills to S$1,500,000; cash bar stalls at S$300,000 and flickers; hatched red gap "FUNDING GAP · S$1,200,000"; "AGREEMENT ≠ FUNDING" slams in with shake and thud | `overlook`, `doesnt`, `cash` |

| 6 CTA | 45.3-57.8 s | don't leave succession to chance; book a review | whole donut with teal protective rim, gear, rising line; "YOUR BUSINESS HAS A FUTURE." -> "DOES YOUR SHAREHOLDER AGREEMENT?"; teal CTA block "BOOK A 15-MINUTE SHAREHOLDER CONTINUITY REVIEW / LINK IN BIO" with a light sweep; disclaimer; fade to navy | `succession`, `book` |

Why it works: a single recurring object (the ownership donut and its dashed orphan half) carries scenes 1, 2 and 6; the bar gauge from scene 4 is called back in scene 5 so the viewer sees the gap get filled, conditionally; each scene ends on a held on-screen message; the climax (scene 4) gets the only shake and the lowest hit in the mix; the music resolves from D minor to major at scene 5.

Wording changes from the brief (meaning unchanged) and compliance copy are in the project's `NARRATION.md`.
