# Compliance wording for financial, insurance and legal explainers

These rules came from the Buy-Sell Agreement brief (Singapore SME owners). They keep a short video persuasive without making claims the licensed entity cannot stand behind. The video is general education; the user's compliance team still signs off before publishing.

1. **Never imply an automatic outcome.** Heirs "may" receive shares; actual outcomes "depend on the company's constitution, shareholder arrangements and applicable law". Do not suggest heirs automatically get unrestricted management rights: say "sharing ownership", not "running the business with them".
2. **Insurance is conditional.** "Properly structured insurance *can help* fund..." and always "subject to policy terms, claims requirements and how the arrangement is set up". Show the conditions on screen (insured event, policy terms met, claim admitted) before any money moves; footnote: "Payouts depend on the insured event, policy terms and claims assessment."
3. **No guarantees, no tax advice.** No promised payouts, no tax or legal conclusions. End card: "General information only. Not financial, legal or tax advice."
4. **Numbers are illustrative** and labelled so ("Illustrative figures only"). Do not use real client or product figures.
5. **Core message, always:** the agreement defines the transfer, insurance may fund it, both must be structured together.
6. **No invented protections or product features.** If a fact is not in the brief or verified, leave it out or hedge it.
7. **CTA is an invitation, not pressure:** "Book a 15-minute shareholder continuity review." No urgency, scarcity or "free" claims unless the user supplies and owns them.
8. **Assets:** only licensed or self-generated media (OFL fonts, Apache-2.0 TTS, synthesised music/SFX). No stock footage, no brand imitation.

Where wording changed from the user's baseline script, record the change and the reason in `NARRATION.md` so the reviewer can see it.
