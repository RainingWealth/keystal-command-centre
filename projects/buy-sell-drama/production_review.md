## 2. Where the film departs from the brief

1. **Photoreal, but stills, not video.** The brief asks for realistic movement and expressive faces. The people in this film are generated photographs that **do not move**: all motion is camera push, drift, light pulses, the paper overlays, and transitions. Spoken lines play over a still face with no lip movement (the brief allows no lip-sync, but not a frozen face). No video-generation model was available: Canva's generators returned `quota_exceeded`, and the other connected tools make stills or product videos only.
2. **Identity between stills is approximate.** No reference images could be passed to the generator, so Aaron, Daniel and Sarah are the same descriptions generated many times; they read as the same kind of person, not the same face (Aaron looks older in some shots than in others). Daniel is kept recognisable by his glasses and navy polo, Aaron by the light blue shirt with rolled sleeves.
3. **Generated, fictional people.** All faces are AI-generated. Two stills were regenerated for not reading as East Asian, one for a different-looking Aaron, one for a blown-out floor (originals in `assets/photo/rejected/`).
4. **Voices.** Kokoro has no Singaporean accent; British voices were used. **I could not listen to any audio.** Voice quality, delivery and accent are unreviewed by ear.
5. **Added lines (3)**, each to meet a requirement: the estate representative's line (the brief asks that Daniel's shares are clearly part of his estate and subject to the legal documents and process), Daniel's "Let's do this properly, while we can.", and Sarah's "Thank you, Aaron." They are marked in `script.md`.
6. **Scene windows moved.** Total is 119.7 s (limit 120). The brief's windows could not hold its own lines at natural speed (S3 alone has about 24 s of speech in a 19 s window): S1 0 to 15.2, S2 15.2 to 26.7, S3 26.7 to 56.4, S4 56.4 to 74.0, S5 74.0 to 79.4, S6 79.4 to 97.6, S7 97.6 to 107.9, S8 107.9 to 119.7.
7. **Step descriptions condensed** from the brief's longer sentences; the meaning is kept ("pays the designated recipient on a covered claim"; "after a valid claim and the legal steps, the survivor buys the shares from the estate").
8. **Rewind uses a framed light box.** The pull-back, rewind and lamp-on shots (74.0 to 79.4 s) show the film inside a wooden frame on a table, drawn in code, with a flipping calendar. It is the one non-photographic sequence in the main cut.
9. **Reading speed.** Reading-time checks use 20 characters/s with 1 s padding, not the library default of 15 + 1.5 s, to fit 120 s. The disclosure is marked fine print and held for 7.5 s.
10. **Six captions are held for less than 1.8 s** (1.28 to 1.76 s) because the next line of speech follows immediately: "But I don't know how to run it.", "So any transfer follows the legal", "The business had value.", "Neither wanted a dispute.", "Thank you, Aaron.", "Daniel was gone."
11. **Name pronunciation.** The voices say "Aaron" the British way (Erin-like); the speech check was told to expect that.
12. **Singapore specifics are generic**: shophouse and HDB-style settings, no real landmark or brand. Company, people and valuation are fictional and labelled illustrative.

## 4. Known weaknesses

- Frozen people: no lip movement or gesture; emotion is carried by the stills, the camera, the music and the cuts.
- Face identity drift between stills (§2.2).
- The "clink" in the toast and the handshakes are in the stills, not performed on the beat.
- The growth montage is two stills; the rewind box is the only non-photographic sequence.
- The end card is sparse on a navy ground; no logo, by design (neutral branding).
- Audio is untested by ear; foley levels (paper, pen, tape whir) were set numerically.

## 5. Improvement opportunities, in order of value

1. **Real motion:** when a video-generation quota is available (Canva or an API key), generate image-to-video clips from these stills, one per shot, and drop them into the same timeline; the audio, captions and cards stay as they are.
2. **Consistent faces:** supply one reference image per character to the image or video generator so the same faces recur.
3. **Human listening pass** and a re-record of narration and dialogue with a real or higher-quality Singaporean-English voice; keep the timeline and re-time the line starts.
4. **Your usual compliance sign-off** on the wording of the three steps and the disclosure. The film does not mention underwriting or exclusions on screen.
5. Cut a 30 to 45 s version for paid social from the same assets (S3 + rewind + S6 + CTA).

## 6. Remaining blockers

- Moving photoreal actors need a video-generation model with quota that this session did not have.
- Release needs a human review of audio, your compliance sign-off, and a decision on the voice.
