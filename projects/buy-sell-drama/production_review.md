## 2. Where the film departs from the brief

1. **No photoreal actors. The brief's central visual requirement is not met.** The client chose Lemo-Opuscar (a code-only film engine with no video model) with the "Paper-cut Lightbox" style. The film is layered backlit paper with silhouette puppets. There are no faces, no skin tones and no visible ethnicity; the brief's "emotional storytelling through facial expressions" is replaced by posture, light, framing and sound. Photoreal generation was not available: the Canva video models needed a paid plan or per-call AI-limit consent (clips of 5 to 15 s, one presenter, no timed text), and no API keys for other services were present.
2. **Renderer substitution.** The library's own three.js scene was replaced by a Canvas 2D paper-stack compositor (same sheet geometry and look) because the container has no GPU (software WebGL measured 2.25 s/frame). Details in `style/STYLE.md`.
3. **Voices.** Kokoro has no Singaporean accent; British voices were used as the closest available. **I could not listen to any audio.** Voice quality, delivery and accent are unreviewed by ear (§3).
4. **Added lines (3)**, each to meet a requirement: the estate representative's line (the brief asks that Daniel's shares are clearly part of his estate and subject to legal documents and process), Daniel's "Let's do this properly, while we can." (the brief shows the advisers' meeting but gives no dialogue), and Sarah's "Thank you, Aaron." They are marked in `script.md`.
5. **Scene windows moved.** Total is 119.7 s (limit 120). The brief's windows could not hold its own lines at natural speed (S3 alone has about 24 s of speech in a 19 s window): S1 0 to 15.2, S2 15.2 to 26.7, S3 26.7 to 56.4, S4 56.4 to 74.0, S5 74.0 to 79.4, S6 79.4 to 97.6, S7 97.6 to 107.9, S8 107.9 to 119.7.
6. **Step descriptions condensed** from the brief's longer sentences; the meaning is kept ("pays the designated recipient on a covered claim"; "after a valid claim and the legal steps, the survivor buys the shares from the estate").
7. **Reading speed.** Reading-time checks use 20 characters/s with 1 s padding, not the library default of 15 + 1.5 s, to fit 120 s. The disclosure is marked fine print and held for 7.5 s.
8. **Six captions are held for less than 1.8 s** (1.28 to 1.76 s) because the next line of speech follows immediately and a cue cannot overlap it: "But I don't know how to run it.", "So any transfer follows the legal", "The business had value.", "Neither wanted a dispute.", "Thank you, Aaron.", "Daniel was gone."
9. **Name pronunciation.** The voices say "Aaron" the British way (Erin-like); the speech check was told to expect that.
10. **Singapore specifics are generic**: shophouse offices, blocks of flats and a generic skyline; nothing is a real landmark or brand. Company, people and valuation are fictional and labelled illustrative.

## 4. Known weaknesses

- Silhouette puppets cannot act with faces; top-down shots (ledger, loan, register) use a plain arm-and-pen shape that looks mechanical.
- Characters share one figure-drawing system, so identity relies on hair, glasses and clothing outlines at small sizes.
- The "clink" in the toast reads as a handshake because the cups are small at that size.
- The end card is sparse on a navy ground; no logo, by design (neutral branding).
- Audio is untested by ear; foley levels (paper, pen, tape whir) were set numerically.

## 5. Improvement opportunities, in order of value

1. **Human listening pass** and a re-record of narration and dialogue with a real or higher-quality Singaporean-English voice (ElevenLabs or a human recording); keep the timeline and re-time the line starts.
2. **Photoreal version:** keep `timeline.json`, `script.md` and the audio; generate character references and shot stills with a photoreal image model, animate hero shots with an image-to-video model, and put them in this timeline as sheets. This needs API access (or a Canva plan with video) that this session did not have.
3. **Compliance review** (licensed adviser, lawyer, MAS advertising rules): wording of the three steps, the disclosure, and whether the end card needs "not advice / subject to underwriting and exclusions". The film does not mention underwriting or exclusions on screen.
4. Add a short beat that shows the company-owned alternative, or a second version for it.
5. Cut a 30 to 45 s version for paid social from the same assets (S3 + rewind + S6 + CTA).
6. Replace the tube-like top-down arm with a proper hand and sleeve, and give each character a recognisable walking and sitting pose set.

## 6. Remaining blockers

- Photoreal footage (brief requirement) needs a photoreal image or video generator with access this session lacked.
- Release needs a human review of audio, a compliance and legal review, and a decision on the voice.
- The Insurance Act s 57 and Companies Act buyback points were not verified against the statutes (the statute site refused automated access).
