# Photoreal stills: provenance

All 25 stills in this folder were generated on 2026-11-23 with Gamma's image generator (`generate_image`, type `photo`, 9:16 "story" preset, 1536×2752), one call each, 70 credits per image. They are AI-generated images of fictional people; no real person, brand or location is depicted, and none is a photograph. No reference images were supplied: character consistency comes only from repeating the same descriptors in every prompt.

Descriptors reused in every prompt: **Aaron** (43, Singaporean Chinese, short black hair with a little grey, light blue shirt, sleeves rolled to the forearms); **Daniel** (45, side-parted black hair, thin black-framed glasses, navy polo); **Sarah** (42, shoulder-length black hair, cream blouse); accountant (woman, fifties, hair in a bun, round glasses, grey cardigan); estate representative (man, fifties, charcoal suit, folder); adviser (woman, forties, dark blazer); warehouse staff in navy polos. Every prompt ends: "No legible text, no logos, no watermark."

Three images were regenerated after review and the first versions kept in `rejected/`: `example` (the first two men did not read as East Asian), `register` (a different, older-looking man in a dark shirt), `sunrise` (a blown-out white band across the floor).
