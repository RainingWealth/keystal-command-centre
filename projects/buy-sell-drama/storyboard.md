# Storyboard: THE PARTNER WHO NEVER CAME BACK

The film is photoreal: each shot is a generated still (`assets/photo/`, prompts in `assets/photo/PROMPTS.md`) with a cinematic camera move, light pulses and a few composited details. **The people in the stills do not move**; all motion is the camera, light and overlays. Timings come from `timeline.json`. An alternate paper-cut version of the same timeline is described in `storyboard_papercut.md`.

## Colour and light

- Hospital: cold teal. Flashback: amber. Present: cool, desaturated, one warm lamp. The plan and the calm outcome: golden daylight. Sunrise: dawn. End card: navy.
- One accent colour (seal red) marks the share, the agreement, "Insufficient" and the step seals, on the paper cards only.

## Characters (recurring descriptors in every prompt)

- Aaron: 43, short black hair with a little grey, light blue shirt with rolled sleeves. Daniel: 45, side-parted hair, thin black glasses, navy polo. Sarah: 42, shoulder-length black hair, cream blouse. Accountant: woman, bun, round glasses, grey cardigan. Estate representative: man, charcoal suit, folder. Adviser: woman, dark blazer.
- Identity between stills is approximate (no reference images could be passed); see production_review.md.
