## Colour and light

- Hospital: cold teal. Flashback: amber and cream. Present: slate blue with one warm lamp. The plan and the calm outcome: gold. Sunrise: dawn orange. End card: navy.
- Back sheets are bright and translucent, front sheets dark; one accent colour (seal red) is used only for the share, the agreement, the "Insufficient" mark and the step seals.

## Characters (silhouette identity)

- Aaron: cropped hair, stocky, rolled sleeves. Daniel: side-parted hair, glasses (cut as a hole so light shows through), a little taller. Sarah: shoulder-length hair, skirt. Accountant: bun, glasses, cardigan. Estate representative and adviser: jackets, slicked hair; the adviser wears glasses.
