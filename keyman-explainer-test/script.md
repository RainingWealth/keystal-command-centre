## Compliance notes
- Payout and covered events are always conditional ("can help", "if an insured key person dies or experiences another covered event", "depending on the policy").
- No insurer name, logo, phone number or guaranteed-outcome claim anywhere.
- Closing fine print on the CTA card: "General information only, not financial advice. Benefits depend on policy terms and conditions."

## Edits to the approved script
- **l04 "Projects stall." → "Your projects stall."** The chosen Kokoro voice blend renders the vowel in a bare "stall" as "stole"; a two-word fragment is also unreliable for speech-to-text. Adding "Your" fixes pronunciation (phoneme override, see `source/lines.json`) and matches the sentence rhythm of the preceding line. Meaning is unchanged.
- All other lines are word-for-word as approved. "KEYMAN" is spoken as the word "Keyman"; the capitals appear only on screen.
